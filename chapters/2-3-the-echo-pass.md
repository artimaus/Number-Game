# Chapter 7 · The Echo Pass

**Arc 2 · The Troll Hills** · Level 7 · numbers 1–20 · 4 tasks per stop · party cap 20 · about 10 sets.

The road climbs into the mountains, through a narrow pass where every sound comes back again… again… again. Two new
tasks arrive: the **first adding** (within 5, with pictures) and the **countdown** ("What comes next?" going
backwards). The troll chief is **Old Rumbletum**, whose tummy rumbles so loudly it echoes for miles. The chapter
also shows that not every troll is grumpy. Conventions are as in [chapter 1](1-1-the-border-road.md) and
[chapter 5](2-1-the-stone-bridge.md).

**The echo:** in this chapter, Buckleberry's numbers come back as a soft echo ("Three!… three… three…"). It's a
sound effect on his recorded or robot voice, so nothing extra needs recording.

## Level 7 tasks
Everything from level 6 stays in the mix (find, count, more or fewer, signs, what comes next, count and compare,
which number is bigger, look closely), plus:

| Task | Numbers | On screen | Cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First adding** (new) | Totals up to 5 | Some things, then more arrive | 3–4 shields |
| **Countdown** (new) | Counting back from up to 10 ("5, 4, 3, _") | Stones going down a slope | 3 numeral stones |

### New: First adding
- **H:** "2 goats… and 1 more! How many now?" `num_2` + `w_goats` + `and` + `num_1` + `more` + `how_many_now`
- **On screen:** 2 goats stand on the path, then 1 more trots in with a hop. The total is never more than 5.
- **Other scenes:** soldiers marching up ("3 soldiers… and 2 more!"), trolls arriving, kites blowing in.
- **Right:** Buckleberry counts them all together: "1, 2, 3. 3 goats!"
- **Help:** count them all together (they light up one by one), then the right shield pulses.
- **Tracked as** the sum, e.g. `+:2+1`.

### New: Countdown
- **H:** "Let's count down! 5, 4, 3… what comes next?" `lets_count_down` + `num_5` + `num_4` + `num_3` + `whats_next`
- **On screen:** numbered stones going down a slope, with the last one blank.
- **Right:** the hero hops down the stones as Buckleberry counts: "5, 4, 3, 2!"
- **In a skirmish,** a right countdown to 1 becomes the charge: "3, 2, 1… Charge!"
- **Tracked as** `back:2`.
