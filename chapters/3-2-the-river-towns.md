# Chapter 10 · The River Towns

**Arc 3 · The Stolen Crown** · Level 10 · adding and taking away within 10 · 5 tasks per stop · army from 4 to 6
companies · about 11 sets.

Beyond the drawbridge lies a green river valley: mill wheels, locks, a harbour full of boats and a lighthouse where the
river meets the sea. River folk whisper that the steward's own boat carried a heavy locked chest up the river. One new
task arrives: **hiding** (how many can't we see?). The captain here is **Captain Plume**, whose helmet feather is so
big it tickles his nose. Conventions are as in [chapter 1](1-1-the-border-road.md), [chapter 5](2-1-the-stone-bridge.md)
and [chapter 9](3-1-the-road-home.md) (guards, companies, forks and troop checks).

### New: Hiding (how many can't we see?)
- **H:** "8 boats… we can see 5. How many are hiding?" `num_8` + `w_boats` + `we_can_see` + `num_5` +
  `how_many_hiding`
- **On screen:** a big "8" on a signpost (the whole). 5 boats float in view; the rest are behind an island (or guards
  behind a wall, fish under a jetty). Under the sign, 8 empty dots; the 5 we can see light up as he counts them.
- **Right:** the hidden ones sail out, one by one: "6, 7, 8! 3 were hiding!" `num_3` + `were_hiding`. Then "5 and 3
  make 8!" `num_5` + `and` + `num_3` + `makes` + `num_8`.
- **Help:** count on together from 5 to 8, pointing at the unlit dots ("6, 7, 8: that's 3!"); then the right shield
  pulses.
- **Why:** it's the part-whole idea (8 is 5 and 3) that makes later adding and taking away easy.
- **Tracked as** `hide:8-5`.
