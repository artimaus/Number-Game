# Chapter 2 · The Whispering Woods

**Arc 1 · The Goblin Border** · Level 2 · numbers 1–5 · 3 tasks per stop · party cap 10 · about 9 sets.

Snagglenose has fled into the woods. The woods are goblin country, so this chapter has more skirmishes and fewer
villages than chapter 1. The special stop, the Owl Tree, gives a treasure but no recruit. Conventions (N:, H:,
[Hero], ★★/★, clip keys) are as in [chapter 1](1-1-the-border-road.md).

## Level 2 tasks

| Task | Numbers | On screen | Cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Find the number** | 1–5 | Banners | 3 banners, with dots |
| **Count them** | 1–5 things in **dice patterns** | In the scene | 3 shields, with dots; wrong choices at least 2 away |
| **Which is more?** | At least **twice** as many (1 vs 2, 2 vs 4, 3 vs 6, up to 8) | Two sides | Tap a side |

## 1 · The Woodcutters' Camp (village)

## 2 · The Owl Tree (special)
*A huge old tree full of holes, with owls peeking out, wide awake and grumpy. Goblin drums lie in the grass.*
- **N:** "In the Owl Tree, the owls were wide awake. The goblins' drums had kept them up all day!" `st1_2_05`
- **H:** "Shh! Let's help the owls sleep." `h_owls_sleep`
- ★★ Count: "How many owls?" `how_many_owls` · ★ More: "Which side has more owls?" `which_more_owls` · ★ Find
- **Each first-try right answer:** an owl pulls on a nightcap and closes its eyes. *Hoo…*
- **End:** the rest of the owls doze off, and the oldest owl drops a **lantern** into the hero's hands.
  - **H:** "A present for our camp!" `h_camp_gift`
  - **N:** "The owls fell fast asleep. The oldest owl gave the army a lantern, and whispered where Snagglenose was
    hiding." `st1_2_06`
