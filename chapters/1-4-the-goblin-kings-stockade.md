# Chapter 4 · The Goblin King's Stockade

**Arc 1 · The Goblin Border** · Level 4 · numbers 1–10 · 3 tasks per stop · party cap 10 · about 10 sets. **Arc
finale.**

Snagglenose has run to his master, the Goblin King **Grubbins the Great**: very round, with a crown too big for him
and a soup ladle for a sceptre. The chapter ends with the biggest siege yet. It's the first to need **ladders and a
battering ram**, a small step up that previews arc 2. Conventions are as in [chapter 1](1-1-the-border-road.md).

## 8 · The Great Stockade (siege, arc finale)
*A tall log wall with two towers, a huge gate, sock flags everywhere. On top: Grubbins the Great, and Snagglenose
beside him.*

**Arrive**
- **N:** "There it was: the Great Stockade! On top stood Grubbins the Great, with Snagglenose beside him."
  `st1_4_15`
- **H:** "The gate is shut tight! We need ladders and a battering ram!" `h_need_ladders_ram`

**Build 1: ladders.** As before; minimum **2**.

**Build 2: the battering ram.**
- **H:** "Now let's build a battering ram!" `h_need_ram`
- **Each first-try right answer** adds the next part, in order: the **log**, the **wheels**, a **roof**, an **iron
  head**. **H:** "A piece of the ram!" `h_ram_part`
- **Minimum: log + wheels.** Short of that: "The ram needs one more piece!" `h_ram_more`, then another round.
- **Ready:** "Ready! Bang the gate!" `h_ram_ready`

**Assault**
- ★★ Count goblins on the wall · ★ More (two towers) · ★ Find
- **Won:**
  - The ram swings: *BOOM… BOOM… CRASH!* The gate falls flat, soldiers swarm the ladders, and a dust cloud rolls
    along the wall.
  - Goblins pour out the back. Grubbins's crown falls off; he grabs it and runs, with Snagglenose behind him.
  - **H:** "Hooray! The fort is ours!" `h_fort_ours`
  - **N:** "Grubbins the Great ran all the way over the mountains, and the goblins never bothered the border
    again." `st1_4_16`
- **Not yet:** retreat. Ladders and ram stay; the next try is a fresh assault round.
